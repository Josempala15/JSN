Why litter matters

Litter doesn’t just stay where it’s dropped. Wind and rain wash it into drains, rivers and eventually the sea, where animals eat it or get tangled up in it. Over 900 different species are known to be affected by rubbish in the sea!

Plastic also breaks down into tiny bits called microplastics that end up in our soil and water. Basically, every bit we pick up helps.

Source: Kühn & van Franeker (2020), Marine Pollution Bulletin – summary.

What you can do

  • Take your rubbish home if the bin’s full.
  • Carry a reusable bottle and bag.
  • Buy second-hand clothes and fix things instead of binning them.
  • See litter you can’t pick up? Snap a photo and report it on the map.
  • Join or organise a clean on the Community cleans tab !

Litter-pick safely

  • Wear gloves and proper shoes, and use a litter picker if you’ve got one.
  • Never touch needles, broken glass or chemicals – report them to your council instead.
  • Stay away from busy roads, steep banks and fast water.
  • Leave nests, plants and animals alone – only take the rubbish.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how JSN collects, uses and protects personal information when you use this website. JSN is run by Jo, who is responsible for the information described below. If you have any questions about this policy or about your information, please contact Josn1505@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information needed to run the website:

  • Account details. The nickname you choose and an account identifier. We do not ask for your name, email address or date of birth. Your recovery code acts as your password and is stored by our database provider in an encrypted form that cannot be read back.
  • Litter reports. The photo you upload, its position on the map, the type of litter, any note you add and the date. Reports, photos and their locations are shown publicly on the map.
  • Cleaning photos. When you mark a spot as cleaned, the photo you provide and the date. This is shown publicly alongside the original report.
  • Community cleans. Cleans you organise, cleans you sign up to and kit you request. The title, area, description and date of a clean are public. The exact meeting point and any contact details are only visible to the organiser and to people who have signed up.
  • Good news posts. Anything you post, which is shown publicly with your nickname.
  • Moderation records. If another user reports that a spot was not actually cleaned, a record is kept of this. These records are only visible to the site administrator.
  • Correspondence. If you email us, for example about a kit request, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message.

3. How we use your information

Your information is used to show litter on the map, to organise community cleans and kit requests, to share good news, to send in-site notifications about cleans, and to prevent misuse of the website. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

4. Lawful basis

We process your information on the basis of our legitimate interests in running a community litter-reporting website that you have chosen to use. Where you contact us by email, we use your details to reply to you.

5. Photos and location

Location is read from your photo on your own device. Before a photo is uploaded it is resized and saved again, which removes the hidden details stored inside it, such as the camera model and the exact time it was taken. If you use the “go to my location” button, your location is used on your device to move the map and is not stored by us. Please do not upload photos showing people’s faces, vehicle number plates or the inside of private homes.

6. Who we share information with

We use the following service providers to run the website. They only process information on our behalf or as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and stores uploaded photos.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website itself.
  • OpenStreetMap, which provides the street map and address search. Your searches and the area of the map you view are sent to them.
  • jsDelivr and unpkg, which deliver some of the code the website uses.

These providers may receive your IP address when your browser connects to them, and some may process information outside the UK under appropriate safeguards. Donations are made through GoFundMe and links to Instagram open their own websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them, and we do not receive your payment details.

7. Information stored on your device

The website stores a small amount of information in your browser so that it works properly. This includes your recovery code, so that you stay signed in, which notifications you have seen, short waiting times between actions, and whether you have closed the welcome message. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account and content remain on the website. You can delete your own reports, kit requests and good news posts at any time. Moderation records are kept for as long as they are needed to prevent misuse. To have your account and all associated information deleted, please email us.

9. Keeping your information secure

Access to the database is controlled by security rules that are enforced by the database itself, so users can only see and change what they are permitted to. Uploads are restricted to images and limited in size and number. However, no website can be completely secure, and you should keep your recovery code private, as anyone who has it can sign in to your account.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how it is used. To make a request, please email us and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Children

If you are under 13, please ask a parent or carer before creating an account. Anyone under 18 should only organise or attend a community clean with a parent, carer or another trusted adult.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page and the date at the top will be updated.
